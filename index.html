Medical Emergency

If someone has passed out, is struggling to breathe, or won't wake up, act immediately.

Step 1: Call 112 (or local emergency lines)

Tell the operator exactly where you are and that you suspect a drug overdose. You will not get in trouble for seeking medical help.

Step 2: Check for Breathing

Roll the person onto their side (the recovery position) so they don't choke while waiting for help to arrive.
